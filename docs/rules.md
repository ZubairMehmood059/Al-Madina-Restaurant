# Project Rules

## 1. Purpose

This project is a restaurant landing and ordering experience for Al Madina Restaurant. The goal is to present the brand clearly, drive reservations and orders, and keep the experience polished and mobile-friendly.

## 2. Core Principles

- Keep the experience modern, warm, and welcoming.
- Prioritize mobile-first design and fast loading.
- Maintain clear navigation and strong calls to action.
- Use accurate restaurant information and consistent brand messaging.
- Keep content concise, readable, and easy to scan.

## 3. Content Rules

- Use the restaurant name consistently as “Al Madina Restaurant”.
- Show real, useful contact and location information.
- Highlight menu categories, reservations, dining experience, and WhatsApp ordering.
- Avoid placeholder or lorem ipsum text.
- Ensure all major sections have clear purpose and value.

## 4. Design Rules

- Use warm, premium color tones that match a hospitality brand.
- Maintain strong spacing and visual hierarchy.
- Keep typography readable and consistent across sections.
- Ensure buttons and links are easy to tap on mobile devices.
- Use imagery and layout elements to support atmosphere and food storytelling.

## 5. Technical Rules

- Keep the app lightweight and performant.
- Prefer simple, maintainable React components.
- Reuse shared data structures instead of duplicating content.
- Keep styling consistent across components.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies or heavy libraries.

## 6. UX Rules

- Every section should help users move toward ordering, reserving, or learning more.
- Make key actions visible without excessive scrolling.
- Use clear labels for reservation, menu, location, and contact actions.
- Ensure accessibility with legible contrast, semantic headings, and proper focus states.

## 7. Quality Bar

- No broken links or missing assets.
- No inconsistent copy or outdated restaurant details.
- No cluttered layouts or low-contrast text.
- No features added without clear user value.

## 8. Review Standard

Before finishing work, check:

- the page looks correct on mobile and desktop,
- key actions are visible and usable,
- information matches the restaurant brand,
- layout remains clean and professional.
